Vet a patent in 30 seconds

Before you license, acquire, design around, or assert a US patent, four calls answer the questions that matter — keyed off a single patent number.

The recipe

1
What is it? — /v1/dossier returns the full picture: claims, citations, family, CPC, current assignee.
2
Is it valid? — /v1/challenges shows whether anyone attacked it at the PTAB, and whether it survived.
3
Is it litigious? — /v1/litigation shows who's been sued over it, where, and over what.
4
Is it alive? — /v1/legal-status confirms it's in force with maintenance fees current (and when it expires).

Verdict in seconds: a battle-tested, heavily-asserted patent held by a known NPE — exactly the risk signal a licensing or FTO team needs before acting.
