AI Patent Search Generator — Claude Connector

Raw USPTO patent intelligence inside Claude: dossiers, claims, prosecution (file-wrapper) history, PTAB validity challenges, district-court litigation, examiner statistics, ownership chain of title, and legal status — fetched live from USPTO Open Data and rendered for chat.

Setup

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors (or find AI Patent Search Generator in the Connector Directory) and add the connector. Custom-URL setup: https://patent-search-generator.web.app/api/mcp.
  2. When prompted, sign in with your email. A one-time code is sent to your inbox — no password needed. New accounts are created automatically and include free starter credits.
  3. In a conversation, enable the connector from the tools menu (next to the message box), then ask a patent question — Claude calls the tools automatically.

Example prompts

  • “Pull the dossier for US10867416B2 and summarize what it covers.”
  • “Has US8724622B2 been challenged at the PTAB? Did it survive?”
  • “Who owns US10867416B2 and is it still in force?”
  • “What happened during prosecution of US10867416B2? Any rejections?”
  • “What's the allowance rate of the examiner who handled US10867416B2?”

Coverage notes

  • US patents only. Data comes from the USPTO Open Data Portal; file-wrapper coverage starts with applications filed in 2001.
  • Litigation data covers 2003–2016 comprehensively and partially to 2020 (the USPTO Patent Litigation Dataset's range). An empty result means no suit on record in that window.
  • All tools are read-only lookups of public USPTO records.

Troubleshooting

  • “Authentication required” / tools missing: make sure the connector is toggled ON for the current conversation (tools menu near the message box), and that you completed the email sign-in when adding it.
  • “Not enough credits”: paid tools are dossier (3, fresh only), claims (1, cold only), litigation (2), examiner (2), and risk_profile (40, fresh only). Check balance; credits can be added from your account. New accounts include free starter credits.
  • “No US patent … found in USPTO ODP”: the patent may pre-date ODP coverage (applications filed before 2001) or the number may be malformed — use the canonical form, e.g. US10867416B2.
  • Disconnect / reconnect: Settings → Connectors → AI Patent Search Generator → Disconnect, then add again and re-run the email sign-in.
