How it works

  • Validity challenges come from the USPTO PTAB Trials API — current, structured, with the petitioner vs. owner and the trial outcome.
  • Infringement litigation comes from the USPTO Patent Litigation Dataset (district-court dockets), pre-indexed for instant per-patent and per-company lookups.
  • Ownership & status (chain of title, in-force, term) come from the USPTO Open Data Portal.
  • Everything keys off a US patent number (or a company name for the reverse lookup). No scraping, no proprietary black box — public-record data you can stand behind.

Example — US 8,724,622

challenges → 15 PTAB IPRs. Microsoft and Apple both challenged it; most were denied institution — the patent survived.
litigation → 31 district-court suits. Uniloc sued Microsoft, Amazon, and Kik over it.
legal-status → in force, maintenance fees current.
One patent number → a complete picture of how battle-tested and litigious it is.

Coverage: US patents only. PTAB, ownership, and prosecution data are current; district-court litigation is comprehensive for 2003–2016 and partial through 2020. Factual public-record reporting — not legal advice.
